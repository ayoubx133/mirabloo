Dash on glowing sky rails, dodge silly trains and play tag in a candy sky city!

About the game

Swipe left and right to switch rails, swipe up or tap to jump and swipe down to roll under candy bars. Run up ramps onto train roofs, grab coin magnets, hoverboards, jetpacks, super sneakers and double coins, and leave the silly conductor and his puppy behind as they slip, trip and get cream pies in the face. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds with a pie blimp boss and sky bonus stages, or chase a high score in endless mode. Finish missions, find 30 stickers, take on the daily challenge and unlock 6 runners and 6 hoverboards.

  • 100 levels in 5 magical 3D worlds plus endless mode
  • Hoverboards, jetpacks, magnets, sneakers and double coins
  • Missions, daily challenge, sticker book and 12 unlockables
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
