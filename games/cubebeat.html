About the game

Beatsy the music cube runs by itself along a glowing 3D neon track, and every spike, block and gap sits right on a beat of the song. Tap to jump in time with the music, bounce on jump pads, tap inside air rings, flip upside down through portals and hold to fly the little rocket. Crash and Beatsy squishes like a pancake, then hops back to the last checkpoint, and practice mode lets you try again with no hearts lost. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, each level with its own brand new song, beat the silly DJ Boombox every 10th level and unlock 6 funky cube skins.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds, each level with its own song
  • Jump pads, air rings, gravity flips, rocket rides and speed portals
  • Kid friendly practice mode, checkpoints and a goofy boss to bonk
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
