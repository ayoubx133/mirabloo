About the game

Explore a cozy magical manor in real 3D, open drawers, chests and wardrobes, and find keys, ladders, screwdrivers, cookies and magic wands to unlock the big front door. Granny wanders the halls humming in her pink curlers, so hide in a wardrobe or under a bed when she spots you. If she catches you she tickles you silly and you wake up back in bed to try again. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, from a cozy cottage to a starlight castle, with a hint button whenever you need help.

  • 100 levels in 5 magical manors
  • Funny granny, a sleepy cat and lots of silly surprises
  • Unlock 6 cozy pajama styles
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
