About the game

Mr. Grumble is the grumpiest neighbor on the street, and it is time for some harmless fun! Sneak into his 3D house while he cooks, reads and naps, grab the prank toys and set them up on his things: a whoopee cushion on his armchair, glue in his hat, slime over the door, pepper in his soup and a frog in his slipper. Then hide in a wardrobe and watch his hilarious reaction, but stay out of his sight or he shoos you out with a broom. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds with a cottage, a garden, a clock house, a snowy chalet and a moonlit manor.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with 18 different silly pranks
  • Sneak, hide, trick him with noisy toys and dodge the nosy parrot
  • Hilarious 3D reactions, a live neighbor cam and 6 prankster kids
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
