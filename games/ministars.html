About the game

Lead your team of mini soccer stars in fast and funny 5v5 matches against cheeky rival clubs. Drag the joystick to run, tap PASS to play a friend and tap SHOOT to score, or tap TACKLE to steal the ball with no fouls and lots of wobbly legs. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, from a sunny park to a moon stadium, with super star shots, jelly bumpers, speed arrows and a cup final every 10th match. Spend your coins on new kits, badges and stadium upgrades, unlock 6 star captains and play 2 players on one tablet.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a cup final every 10th match
  • Build your club: kits, badges, stadium upgrades and a trophy room
  • 2 players on one tablet and 6 star captains to unlock
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
