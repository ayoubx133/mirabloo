Poke, tickle and splat cute 3D planets with silly space toys!

About the game

Pick a silly space toy and poke a cute planet with a face: splat jelly meteors, blow bubbles, paint rainbow stripes, tickle it with a giant feather, fly a comet around it, drop bouncy moons, sprinkle stars, send snow clouds or plant flowers. Planets wobble like jelly, giggle, sneeze, get dizzy and change seasons, and one shake makes them sparkly clean again, so nothing is ever broken. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds of space challenges, cheer up a grumpy giant planet every 10th level and finish 3 daily tasks for bonus coins. Collect 30 planet friends and 10 giants, dress them up with hats, rings, glasses and orbiting buddies, and unlock 6 hero planet skins.

  • 10 silly space toys: jelly meteors, bubbles, rainbow ray, feather, comet and more
  • 100 levels in 5 worlds, grumpy giants to cheer up and 3 daily tasks
  • Collect and dress up 40 planet friends that giggle, sneeze and always reform
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
