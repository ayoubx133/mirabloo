About the game

Tap anywhere to make your little ninja run there, and stay out of the flashlight cones. Sneak up behind each sleepy guard and tag him with your glowing star wand: he spins, flops over and snores while a shower of gems flies to you. Dodge peeky cameras, blinking tickle beams, sniffy puppies and squeaky ducks, hop through magic portals and outsmart Captain Snooze in a boss level every 10 levels. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds and unlock 6 ninja outfits with extra hearts and speed.
