About the game

Step into a magical 3D arena as a brave stick hero with a glowing aura. Move with the joystick, tap to punch, kick and dash, jump for flying kicks and hold the power button to charge up. When your power is full, tap it for an AURA BURST: you transform with a new aura color and fire a mega beam! Silly foes never get hurt, they spin away and poof into stars. Fight 100 levels in 5 worlds with a boss every 10th fight, unlock 6 heroes and play 2 players on the same tablet.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a boss every 10th fight
  • Charge your aura, transform and fire mega beams
  • 2 players on one tablet and 6 heroes to unlock
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
