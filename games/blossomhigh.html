Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Explore a magical 3D school and town, make friends, play classes and adopt pets!

About the game

Walk, run and double jump around a cute 3D school and a cozy town full of cherry blossoms, from the rooftop garden to the festival square. Join fun classes that are mini games: pop math bubbles, paint pixel pictures, tap music beats, race on the track and cook silly recipes. Chat with 8 funny friends, give gifts and high fives to fill their friendship hearts, join a club, adopt a pet and change your hairstyle and outfit in the closet. Finish 64 quests, 3 daily tasks and a sticker book, take photos, and watch the fireworks when the day turns into a starry night.

  • Open 3D school and town with day and night
  • 5 class mini games, 8 friends, 7 pets and 64 quests
  • Closet with hairstyles, colors, accessories and 6 uniforms
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
