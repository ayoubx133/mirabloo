About the game

Join GRINNY and his buddies for goofy 3v3 water splash matches against silly rival teams. Drag the left stick to run, drag the right stick to aim and let go to splash, or tap it to auto aim, and tap SUPER for a big move like a bubble storm or a mega balloon. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds with Splash Count, Gem Grab and Ball Race, hide in bushes, bounce on jelly pads, slide on slippy puddles and hop through bubble portals, with a Big Ducky boss every 10th match. Nobody gets hurt: soaked buddies do a wet dog shake and pop right back, and the losing team gets a giant bucket of water!

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with 3 match modes and a Big Ducky boss every 10th match
  • 6 buddies to unlock, each with a toy splasher and a super move
  • Easy twin stick controls with auto aim, made for kids
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
