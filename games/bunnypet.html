Care for Bloo, a fluffy 3D bunny who giggles, sneezes and copies your taps!

About the game

Tap Bloo's nose, belly, ears and feet for silly reactions, and tap a few times in a row to watch her copy you with her own paws and a squeaky voice. Feed her 16 snacks with funny surprises like hot pepper fire, brain freeze ice cream and onion tears, give her bubble baths, brush her teeth, tuck her into bed and grow carrots in her garden. Dress her up with 52 hats, glasses, bows, outfits and shoes, decorate her living room, play 3 mini games and travel to 5 magical places to find golden carrots. Follow 60 quests in 6 chapters plus 3 new daily tasks, and level up your bunny friend.

  • A fluffy 3D bunny with dozens of funny reactions
  • 60 quests, daily tasks, 3 mini games and 5 trips
  • Dress up with 52 items and 6 fur colors
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
