Build candy towers, pop silly goos and save the queen's cupcakes!

About the game

Silly goo creatures are hopping down the cookie road to snack on Queen Candela's cupcakes. Tap a cookie spot to build lollipop shooters, gumball poppers, cotton clouds, chocolate walls and rainbow beams, then upgrade them to pop every goo into sprinkles. Tap the ground to walk the queen into battle and use her Royal Shout to make the goos dizzy. Play 100 levels in 5 candy worlds with a giant goo boss every 10th level.

  • 100 levels in 5 candy worlds with a goo boss every 10th level
  • 5 candy towers with 3 upgrades each and a brave queen hero
  • Funny goos, Royal Shout, x2 speed and 6 queen outfits
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
