Play pretend in a magical 3D doll town: dress up, cook, find surprises!

About the game

Drag Minty, Pumpky the giggly pumpkin and their friends between a cozy home, a star salon, a happy hospital, a rainbow school, a cupcake cafe and a magic park on a floating island. Every object reacts when you tap it: open the fridge, cook eggs and bake cakes, give bubble baths, swing, slide and bounce, and feed your friends for silly reactions like hot pepper fire puffs and brain freeze shivers. Dress up your dolls with 50 hairstyles, colors, outfits, hats and shoes, find 18 hidden surprises and fill your sticker book. Follow 54 story quests in 6 chapters plus 3 new daily tasks, switch to a starry magic night, and your town is saved just the way you left it.

  • 6 magical 3D places to explore and play pretend in
  • 54 story quests, daily tasks and 18 hidden surprises
  • Dress up with 50 items and 6 special looks
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
