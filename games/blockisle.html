About the game

Move with the stick, tap a block to mine it, pick a block in your hotbar and tap to build anything you can dream of. Craft magic pickaxes, doors, beds, lanterns, glass, paint and a shining star beacon, then build bridges across the sky to unlock 5 floating islands: Meadow, Candy, Crystal, Honey and Star Isle. Bounce on jelly slimes, pet hoppy bunnies, catch butterflies, climb a giant beanstalk and nap until morning while lanterns glow at night. Finish 60 quests and 3 new daily tasks every day, or switch to Creative Mode to build with every block and fly.

  • 5 magical floating islands with 60 quests and daily tasks
  • 30+ blocks to mine, craft and build, plus Creative Mode
  • Day and night, friendly bunnies, bouncy slimes and a sheep cloud
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
