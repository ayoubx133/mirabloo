About the game

Help a cuddly teddy explorer bring the lost star pieces home! Run, jump, double jump, glide on a big leaf and roll through snow across 5 dreamy islands: Flower Meadow, Candy Forest, Snow Peaks, Cloud Castle and Moon Fair. Every island has 20 star missions: race through rings, grab big shiny buttons, bounce silly goo blobs, help funny villagers find their lost things and bounce the Goo King every 10th level. 100 levels in 5 worlds with an easy joystick, big buttons and a friendly Bee Taxi that catches every fall.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds of open 3D islands
  • Jump, double jump, leaf glide, roll and ride the wind
  • 6 cute teddy skins, silly villagers and goo bosses
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
