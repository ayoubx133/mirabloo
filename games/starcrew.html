About the game

Lead a cute crew of space animals in bubble helmets as the bunny captain of a colorful 3D starship. Walk around with the joystick and play fun task mini games: fix the wires, draw star maps, water space plants, repair the robot, fuel the engine and sort space fruit. One crewmate is a sneaky prankster who goos your tasks, so follow goo footprints, grab clues and talk to witnesses, then press the big button and vote him into the bouncy room. Play 100 missions in 5 worlds with lights out, goo doors, goo balloons, chase missions and two pranksters at once, plus daily tasks and 6 bunny captain suits to unlock.

  • 100 funny missions in 5 starship worlds, with a chase mission every 10 levels
  • 7 task mini games, clues, witnesses and silly votes with a squeaky rubber duck
  • Cute space animal crew, daily tasks, no violence and safe for kids
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
