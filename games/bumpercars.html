About the game

Drive GOLDIE, a grinning golden bumper car, through 100 levels in 5 worlds of a magical toy city at night. Tap the arrows to steer, hold GO to zoom and bonk the other cars to win bumper derbies, races, crown zone battles, star hunts and giant bowling pin events. Grab gift boxes for turbo, bubble shields, boing horns, jelly slicks and star magnets, bounce over ramps and jump pads, and bonk a giant boss car when he gets dizzy every 10th level. Earn coins to unlock 6 funny cars, paint, faces and hats in the garage.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds: derbies, races, crown zones, star hunts and giant bowling pins
  • Silly bumper cars with faces, kid drivers, dizzy bonks and 5 funny power-ups
  • Garage with 6 cars plus paint, faces and hats, a boss every 10th level
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
