About the game

Grab a corner of the tablet: every player has ONE big button, so anyone can join the party in seconds. Hold to walk and let go to spin, tap to hop, hold to turn your kart or duck boat, and tap at just the right moment in 32 funny 3D mini games like sumo splash, giggle bomb, kart craze, paint rollers, candy trails and puff soccer. Play the tour alone against cheeky CPU pals through 100 levels in 5 worlds with a party cup every 10 levels, or play a random party, pick a favorite game or roll the dice on the party board with up to 4 friends. Win trophies, earn coins and dress your red pal in hats like a party hat, bunny ears, a royal crown or a melon head.

  • 1 to 4 players on one tablet with one big corner button each
  • 32 mini games, 100 levels in 5 worlds, party board and trophies
  • Silly reactions, rubber duck splashes and 8 fun hats to collect
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
