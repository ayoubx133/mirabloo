Roll a shiny star ball over twisty space tracks in 100 silly, cosmic levels!

About the game

Hold your finger to roll the shiny star ball and drag left and right to steer it along twisting tracks, from sunny wooden sky roads all the way up to tracks floating between the planets. Bounce off bumpers, zoom over boost pads, fly from jump pads, loop the loop and time your roll past spinners, sliding blocks and blinky gates. If you roll off the edge, a little smiley rocket swoops in and carries you back to the last checkpoint with a funny line. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, beat a silly space boss every 10th level and collect coins for 6 cool balls.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds, from sunny sky roads to space, with a boss every 10th level
  • Easy hold and drag controls, a rocket buddy always brings you back
  • 6 shiny balls to unlock, loops, jump pads, spinners and more
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
