Walk your doll through fashion gates, match the look and wow the judges!

About the game

Drag left and right to walk your doll through magic fashion gates and pick outfits, hairstyles, shoes and hats that match the target look on your card. Paint gates change colours, stinky cheese makes you smelly until a bubble bath cleans you, and silly gates turn you into a banana, a box robot or a girl with broccoli hair. At the end of the catwalk you strike a pose on the runway and the animal judges hold up their score cards. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, beat the show off rival FIFI in the Grand Shows and unlock 6 dolls.

  • 100 levels in 5 dreamy worlds with a Grand Show every 10th level
  • Over 40 outfits, hairstyles, shoes and hats, including very silly ones
  • Runway show with funny animal judges and 6 dolls to unlock
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
