About the game

Ride the smiling cloud ship over a sunny island, jump out and glide down on your parachute. Pop gift crates to find paint blasters, bubble shields, sponges and balloon bombs, then splat the rival teams with bright paint while the rainbow zone shrinks. Nobody gets hurt: a painted player pops into confetti and floats away in a bubble to watch. Be the last clean team on 5 magical islands with 100 levels in 5 worlds and a giant goo boss every 10th match.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a silly goo boss every 10th match
  • Parachute drops, gift crates, 5 paint blasters and bubble shields
  • Kid friendly paint battles with 6 fun heroes to unlock
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
