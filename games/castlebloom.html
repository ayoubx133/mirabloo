About the game

Tap groups of two or more matching jellies to blast them and reach each level goal before your moves run out. Big groups make rockets, poppers and bloom orbs, and boosters that touch make giant combos. Every win earns stars that rebuild King Bloomworth's floating kingdom, one building at a time, with fireworks and cheering pets. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds with hedges, gift boxes, soap bubbles, chicks to bring home, sleepy fuzz and a silly boss every 10th level.

  • 100 levels in 5 magical worlds with a funny boss every 10th level
  • Rockets, poppers, bloom orbs and giant booster combos
  • Rebuild a floating 3D kingdom with 25 buildings to unlock
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
