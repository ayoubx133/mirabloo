Toy Town Parking 3D

FamilyFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Drive cute toy cars with funny faces and park them in a magical 3D toy town!

About the game

Steer the big toy wheel, press GO and BACK, and park your smiling toy car in the glowing spot. Bump a cone and it says BOINK, honk the horn and the sleepy cars wake up, then watch your car wink when you park just right. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds: Block Town, Candy Downtown, Toy Harbor with ramp jumps, Dino Toy Park with trailers and Moon Base with floaty jumps, plus giant toy truck and night light special levels. Drive around the toy town in Free Drive to collect stars, then paint your cars, swap wheels and add stickers in the garage.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with special truck and night levels
  • 6 toy cars to unlock with paint, wheels and stickers
  • Free Drive toy town with jumps and stars to collect
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
