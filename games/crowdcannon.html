About the game

Drag left and right to aim your candy cannon and fire a happy stream of tiny jelly buddies down a floating 3D track. Steer them through glowing x2, x3 and +10 gates to multiply the crowd, dodge the sour gates and spinny lollipops, and pop the grumpy goo blobs on the way. Swarm the worried goo fortress until it melts into a giggly puddle, launch a MEGA buddy when the meter is full, and beat a giant silly goo boss every 10th level. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, earn coins, power up your cannon and unlock 6 colorful cannons.

  • 100 levels in 5 magical worlds with a giant goo boss every 10th level
  • Hundreds of cute jelly buddies, x2 x3 and +10 gates, MEGA buddy shots
  • 6 candy cannons to unlock plus speed, power and shield upgrades
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
