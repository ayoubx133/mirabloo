Run, jump and bounce up magical sky towers in 3D. 100 levels in 5 worlds!

About the game

Climb a magical tower in the clouds! Move with the joystick, tap the big button to jump and drag the screen to look around. Hop across candy blocks, bounce on jelly cubes and jump pads, ride moving platforms, dodge swinging candy hammers and float through star bubbles. If you fall, Puffy the cloud catches you and flies you back to your checkpoint. There are 100 levels in 5 worlds with a silly giant to beat every 20 levels, plus coins to collect for 6 fun skins with sparkly trails.

  • 100 levels in 5 magical worlds
  • Kid friendly controls, no hurt, Puffy saves every fall
  • 6 skins with trails, giants and race levels
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
