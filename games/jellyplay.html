Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Grab, fling and bounce silly jelly buddies in 5 magical 3D playgrounds!

About the game

Pick up a wobbly jelly buddy with your finger, carry it anywhere and flick to fling it across the room. Bounce on trampolines, ride slides, spinners and conveyor belts, launch from pillow cannons and rocket pads, float away with balloons or get caught in a giant bubble. Open the toy box to add 70 toys, treats and hats, then feed your buddies, dress them up or make them giant or tiny with the magic wand. Finish 60 fun challenges and 3 daily tasks to unlock new toys, arenas and buddy skins. Buddies never get hurt: they wobble, giggle, see silly stars and pop right back up.

  • Real 3D squishy jelly physics: grab, fling, stack and bounce
  • 5 magical arenas, 70 toys, 60 challenges plus daily tasks
  • Silly reactions everywhere: giggles, burps, sneezes and dizzy stars
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
