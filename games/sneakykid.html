About the game

It is a magical night and you want to go play outside, but the whole family is still awake! Move with the stick, tiptoe past the soft light cones, hide in wardrobes and bushes, and grab your backpack, skateboard and keys before you sneak out the door. Throw squeaky ducks to distract the nosy puppy, switch off the TV so Dad goes to fix it, and tiptoe over creaky floors while Grandpa naps. Get caught and it is a funny AHA! and back to your room, so try again and be extra sneaky in 100 levels in 5 worlds.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds: cozy house, moonlit garden, toy garage, grandma's cottage and school night
  • Hide, tiptoe, throw toys, turn off the TV and dodge flashlights
  • Funny family reactions, 6 kid outfits and a magical night in 3D
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
