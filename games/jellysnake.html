Steer a jelly snake, eat glowing dots, grow big and boop smaller snakes!

About the game

Drag anywhere to steer your wobbly jelly snake around a magical floating arena. Eat glowing dots to grow bigger, then touch smaller snakes to boop them into a shower of candy, but keep away from snakes with a bigger number. Collect stars, become number one before the timer ends, zoom with boost, pop power bubbles and tickle giant crowned boss snakes on their glowing tails. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, from Berry Meadow to Starlight Dream, and collect 6 snake skins.

  • 100 levels in 5 magical worlds with a silly boss snake every 10 levels
  • Grow, boop and race to number one in real 3D jelly arenas
  • Boost, power bubbles, bouncy bumpers, magic portals and 6 snake skins
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
