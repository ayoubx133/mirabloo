Fairy Castle Match 3D

FamilyFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Swap glossy gems, help a fairy queen and restore her magical castle!

About the game

Swipe two neighbor gems to line up 3 or more of the same kind and reach each level goal before your moves run out. Match 4 for a rocket, a square for a butterfly, an L shape for a pixie bomb and 5 for a rainbow, then swap boosters together for giant combos. Every win earns stars that restore the fairy castle room by room, and you choose a royal, sweet or silly style for every piece of decor. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds with gift boxes, magic vines, ice blocks, sticky honey, crystal locks and a grumpy little cloud boss every 10th level.

  • 100 levels in 5 magical worlds with a silly cloud boss every 10th level
  • Rockets, butterflies, pixie bombs, rainbows and big booster combos
  • Restore 10 castle rooms and pick royal, sweet or silly decor
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
