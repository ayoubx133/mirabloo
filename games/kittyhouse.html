Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Be a cheeky 3D kitty: jump, knock things over, nap and escape the silly granny!

About the game

Run, jump and climb through five big magical 3D houses as a fluffy little kitty. Knock teacups off tables, nibble cookies, nap in sunny beds, play the piano with your paws and bounce on jelly cushions. A sleepy granny with a feather duster tries to tickle you back to bed, so hide in a box or jump up high where she cannot reach. Finish 60 funny mischief quests and fresh daily tasks to open new houses and dress up six cute kitties.

  • 5 magical 3D houses to explore: cottage, candy villa, treehouse, cloud castle and moon mansion
  • 60 silly mischief quests plus 3 new daily tasks every day
  • 6 cute kitties with hats, funny granny chases and hide and seek
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
